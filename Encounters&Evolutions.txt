This is a list of encounters that are added or adjusted
and how certain pokemon evolve in Pokemon Patched Crystal.


~~Restored Encounters~~

Vulpix can be found during the morning and day at:
Route 36, 37, 7, 8 

Mankey can be found rarely during the morning and mostly during the day at:
Route 42, 9

Primeape can be found rarely during the night at:
Route 9

Mareep can be found at:
Route 32, 42, 43 

Flaaffy can be found during the night at:
Route 42, 43

Girafarig can be found rarely during the morning and day at:
Route 43 

Remoraid and Octillery can be found at most bodies of water using both rods and SURF.

Chikorita can be found only during morning at:
Route 31 

Totodile can be found only during the day at:
Route 31

Cyndaquil can be found only during the night at:
Dark cave (Violet city entrance) 

Bulbasaur can be found only during morning and night at:
Ilex Forest

Squirtle can be found at:
Route 34 (Surf)

Charmander can be found only during the night at:
Burned Tower B1F

Omanyte, Kabuto and Aerodactyl fossils can be found by smashing rocks and revived
at the Ruins of Alph Research Center.


~~Additional Encounters~~

Skarmory can be found during the day at:
Route 38

Smeargle can be found during the day at:
Sprout Tower 3F

Mantine can be found at:
Cianwood, Olivine City (Surf)

Corsola can be found at:
Route 34, Olivine City (Surf)

Larvitar can be found rarely during the day at:
Route 42

Misdreavus can be found rarely during the night at:
Sprout/Tin Tower, Route 31, 32

Houndour can be found rarely during the day and mostly during the night at:
Route 36, 37

Murkrow can be found during the night at:
Route 38 

Slugma can be found during the day at:
Burned Tower B1F 


~~Legendaries~~

Celebi can be found through the GS Ball event after beating the Elite Four

Zapdos can be found as an overworld encounter at:
Rock Tunnel

Moltres can be found as an overworld encounter at:
Victory Road

Articuno can be found as an overworld encounter at:
Ice Path

Mewtwo can be found as an overworld encounter at:
Silver Cave

Mew can be found as an overworld encounter at:
The 4th secret Unown inscription chamber at the Ruins of Alph.
To access this chamber you need to have Ho-Oh in your party.


~~Evolutions~~

Haunter, Machoke, Graveler, Kadabra, Seadra, Onix, Scyther evolve by using a Link Cord

Slowpoke evolves into Slowking by using a Link Cord

Poliwhirl evolves into Politoed by using a Link Cord

Eevee evolves into Espeon by using a Sun Stone

Eevee evolves into Umbreon by using a Moon Stone

Porygon evolves into Porygon2 by using an Up-grade
